## Custom full-sized keyboard

This project started when I set out to build a custom mechanical keyboard and tried to find a full-sized custom PCB.
I quickly realized that full-sized custom layouts aren't nearly as popular as smaller form factors, leaving very few options on the market.
Instead of compromising on the layout, I decided to design and build one myself from scratch.

## Nothing more permanent than temporary

It isn't finished, but I realized waiting for perfection meant never documenting it at all.
Despite a few capacitor mistakes and minor key-ghosting, it is currently my daily driver.
I am proud of how far it has come, so I wanted to share it.
You can find the source files on [my GitHub](https://github.com/NBalciunas/Skeleton104).
